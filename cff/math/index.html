The formulas (same example as the video)

1 · Read a grid with the coordinate scale

Right: where the square's left line crosses the bottom scale
Up: where the point sits on the side scale
26 + 5.4 → 2654 · 42 + 6.8 → 4268 → GA 2654 4268

The trainer lays the scale for you: "◎ OP" and "✕ Plot" under the map.

2 · Range from the mil relation

W = R × M → R = W ÷ M × 1000
7.4 m ÷ 5 mils × 1000 = 1,480 m

W is given to you by the cadre before you start (width and height): you never measure it. You measure M: press Measure in the binoculars and read the small scale at the left (width on the bottom, height on the side), then use the matching W.

3 · Magnetic → grid direction

G-M angle EAST: add · WEST: subtract
mils: 0300 + 120 = 0420 (to the nearest 10)
degrees: 16.9° + 6.8° = 23.6° × 17.78 = 420 mils

Read the compass: press Compass in the trainer, tap the vehicles, read the dial under the line (degrees outside, mils inside).

4 · Target location (polar plot)

θ° = mils × 0.05625 (6400 mils = 360°)
ΔE = R × sin θ = 1480 × 0.40 = 593
ΔN = R × cos θ = 1480 × 0.92 = 1356
E 26540 + 593 = 27133 · N 42680 + 1356 = 44036 → GA 2713 4403

On paper: protractor on the OP, mark the direction, measure the range, read the point. In the trainer: the Plot button.

5 · Deviation correction

OT factor = range in thousands, rounded (1,480 → 1)
meters = mils × OT factor, to the nearest 10 (32 × 1 → 30)
burst RIGHT → send LEFT · under 30 m → leave it out

6 · Range correction (successive bracketing)

SHORT → ADD · OVER → DROP
400 → 200 → 100 → 50 + FIRE FOR EFFECT

First jump 400 (800 if the burst is farther off). Once the target is between two rounds, halve the jump every round.

The worksheet order is a recommendation. For the radio you need the target grid; the other lines are how you get it.
