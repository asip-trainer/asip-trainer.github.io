        Cheat sheet · Call for fire (CFFT)

        ▶ Watch: the math, step by step (video, 5:05)

        Phase 1 · Locate the target (worksheet)

        1. Observer location: put the protractor on the OP (button under the map) and read right, then up: where the square's left grid line crosses the bottom scale, then where the point sits on the side scale. 8 digits = 10 m.
        2. Target: through the binoculars, count the vehicles and name them (BMP, T-72, truck).
        3. Apparent size M: press Measure in the binoculars: one vehicle sits in the corner of the small scale at the left. Width on the bottom scale, height on the side scale (1 mil per tick).
        4. Range (WORM): W = R × M, so R = W ÷ M × 1000. W is given to you by the cadre before you start (width or height); you only measure M. Example: 7.4 m, M = 5 mils → 7.4 ÷ 5 × 1000 = 1,480 m.
        5. Compass direction: press Compass, tap the middle of the group, read the dial under the line: degrees on the outer ring, mils on the red inner ring.
        6. Grid direction: magnetic → grid: G-M angle EAST: add, WEST: subtract. Degrees × 17.78 = mils (6400 mils = 360°). Send direction in mils, to the nearest 10.
        7. Target location: polar plot. From the OP, lay the grid direction and measure the range (the map's Plot tool does the protractor and ruler for you); read the grid at the mark. It must be within 250 m.

        Phase 2 · Radio

        The radio is already on the fire net. Check call signs: the FDC's first, then yours. The six elements: observer identification, warning order, target location, target description, method of engagement, method of fire and control.

        Phase 3 · Call for fire: one transmission

        Z57, THIS IS H44, ADJUST FIRE, GRID 4523 9671, 3 BMPs IN THE OPEN, ICM, WHEN READY, OVER

        In order: observer ID, warning order, target location, target description, method of engagement, method of fire and control. OVER is added when you press Transmit. The FDC reads it back, then sends the MTO (unit, rounds, target number), SHOT and SPLASH (5 s before impact): nothing to answer, just watch the target.

        Phase 4 · Adjust (successive bracketing)

        • Spot each burst: left or right of the target in mils (reticle), and SHORT (in front, lower) or OVER (behind, higher).
        • Deviation: mils × OT factor (range in km, rounded: 1,490 m → 1). Round to 10 m. Under 30 m: leave it out. Burst right → LEFT.
        • Range: short → ADD, over → DROP. First 400, then halve each round once you have a bracket: 200, 100, then 50 + FIRE FOR EFFECT. Not bracketed yet? Repeat (or double) until the round crosses the target.
        • Grid mission: send DIRECTION #### once, with the first correction. Order: direction, deviation, range, FIRE FOR EFFECT. Example: DIRECTION 0860, LEFT 40, ADD 400, OVER.

        Phase 5 · End of mission

        After ROUNDS COMPLETE: END OF MISSION, TARGET DESTROYED, 2 CASUALTIES, OVER. Report what you actually see: destroyed (every vehicle burning), damaged (some), or no effect.
