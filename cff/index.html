        Cheat sheet · Call for fire (CFFT)

        Phase 1 · Locate the target (worksheet)

        1. Observer location: put the protractor on the OP (button under the map) and read right, then up: where the square's left grid line crosses the bottom scale, then where the point sits on the side scale. 8 digits = 10 m.
        2. Target: through the binoculars, count the vehicles and name them (BMP, T-72, truck).
        3. Apparent size M: the width of one vehicle, end to end, on the reticle's horizontal scale (ticks every 5 mils, numbers every 10). Read to half a mil.
        4. Range (WORM): W = R × M, so R = W ÷ M × 1000. Example: BMP 6.7 m, M = 4.5 mils → 6.7 ÷ 4.5 × 1000 ≈ 1,490 m.
        5. Magnetic direction: put the reticle center on the middle of the group and read the compass (MAG box), in mils.
        6. Grid direction: magnetic → grid: G-M angle EAST: add, WEST: subtract. Send direction to the nearest 10 mils.
        7. Target location: polar plot. From the OP, lay the grid direction and measure the range (the map's Plot tool does the protractor and ruler for you); read the grid at the mark. It must be within 250 m.

        Phase 2 · Radio

        Set the fire-net frequency (5 digits, e.g. 36550 = 36.550). Check call signs: the FDC's first, then yours. The six elements: observer identification, warning order, target location, target description, method of engagement, method of fire and control.

        Phase 3 · Call for fire: three transmissions

        1. Z57, THIS IS H44, ADJUST FIRE, OVER (observer ID + warning order)
        2. GRID 4523 9671, OVER (target location)
        3. 3 BMPs IN THE OPEN, ICM, WHEN READY, OVER (description, method of engagement, method of fire and control)

        The FDC reads each one back. Then the message to observer (MTO): unit to fire, rounds, target number. Read it back. SHOT, OVER → SHOT, OUT. SPLASH, OVER (5 s before impact) → SPLASH, OUT and watch the target.

        Phase 4 · Adjust (successive bracketing)

        • Spot each burst: left or right of the target in mils (reticle), and SHORT (in front, lower) or OVER (behind, higher).
        • Deviation: mils × OT factor (range in km, rounded: 1,490 m → 1). Round to 10 m. Under 30 m: leave it out. Burst right → LEFT.
        • Range: short → ADD, over → DROP. First 400, then halve each round once you have a bracket: 200, 100, then 50 + FIRE FOR EFFECT. Not bracketed yet? Repeat (or double) until the round crosses the target.
        • Grid mission: send DIRECTION #### once, with the first correction. Order: direction, deviation, range, FIRE FOR EFFECT. Example: DIRECTION 0860, LEFT 40, ADD 400, OVER.

        Phase 5 · End of mission

        After ROUNDS COMPLETE, OVER: END OF MISSION, TARGET DESTROYED, 2 CASUALTIES, OVER. Report what you actually see: destroyed (every vehicle burning), damaged (some), or no effect.
