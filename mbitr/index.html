Score sheet · Operate an MBITR Radio

    The voice part (radio check in the net) and the stopping procedures are not part of this key drill.

    Exam mode

    • One clock for the whole drill: 2:00. It starts when you press Start.
    • No coach, no key highlights, no ✓ marks until the end.
    • Assemble the radio yourself: tap the battery, the antenna and the hand mic to put them on. No battery, no power. A part still missing at the end is a NO-GO.
    • The radio comes as in the test: off, with the battery, antenna and hand mic beside it, and the channel switch on 1, and every setting at the factory default (BASIC, PLAIN, 5 W, internal audio).
    • Volume knob clockwise → wait for the self-test → ALT + MENU → PROGRAM → CHANNEL → check PLAIN and PWR 5.0W → SINCGARS (ENT jumps to FX) → FX: ENT, ▲/▼ per digit, ALT + ▲ to the next slot on the right, ENT → ESC 3 times → MENU → TOP AUDIO → ENT → ESC. The antenna and hand mic can go on at any point. Any order works and slips can be fixed; only the final settings count. ALT counts only while held, as on the radio.
    • The exam ends when channel 1 is set, the audio is TOP AUDIO and you are back at the main display, or at 2:00. A NO-GO sends you to Charlie Company.

    Cheat sheet · AN/PRC-148 MBITR (JEM)

    The keypad

    Seven keys. Top row MODE GR ESC, with MENU SCAN LOCK printed above them: those are their ALT functions. Then ▼ ▲ ENT (LAMP printed below ENT), and ALT. As on the radio, ALT works only while it is held: on a phone keep one finger on ALT and tap the other key with a second finger; on a computer hold the A key (or Alt/Option) and click or press the other key. ALT on its own does nothing.

    Score-sheet steps and the keys for each (one channel)

    1. Place MBITR Radio into operation.
      • Install battery: twist it onto the bottom until the latch locks.
      • Turn the volume knob (top panel, left) clockwise out of OFF. The radio turns on.
      • Wait for the self-test: TESTING, then the main screen (G01 / CHAN001 / PT FM). Keys do nothing until then.
      • Attach the antenna (thread it on, hand tight) and plug the hand mic into the audio connector on top. This can be done at any point, even at the end.
    2. Load a frequency and properly enter the net.
      • Start up: the channel switch (top panel, middle) comes on 1; with group G01 that is CHAN001. On the CHANNEL screen, turning it picks the channel being programmed, even while you are entering a frequency (a change not yet confirmed with ENT is dropped).
      • Programming: ALT + MODE (MENU) opens the main menu → ▼ twice to PROGRAM → ENT → ▼ once to CHANNEL → ENT. The screen opens on CH = 001.
      • Operating system: ▼ to the bottom line (BASIC) → ENT → ▼ to SINCGARS → ENT. The radio jumps straight to FX on screen 2.
      • Net information: check PLAIN (line 1, right) and PWR=5.0W (line 3); both are the factory defaults, so they are usually right already. For any field: move the outline with ▲/▼, ENT to change it (it turns dark), ▲/▼ to pick, ENT to confirm.
      • Frequency: the outline is already on FX = ##.### (screen 2, under SINCGAR = MAN and ECCM = SC) → ENT: the first digit turns dark. ▲/▼ change that digit; ALT + ▲ moves to the next slot on the right, ALT + ▼ back to the left. ENT to confirm. 81.000 from 67.500: 6 → 8, ALT + ▲, 7 → 1, ALT + ▲, 5 → 0; the last two digits are already 0, so ENT.
      • Return: ESC 3 times: CHANNEL → PROGRAM → main menu → main display, which now shows PT SG SC.
      • Audio for the hand mic: MODE (MENU, no ALT) → INTERNAL AUDIO is outlined → ENT → ▼ → TOP AUDIO → ENT → ESC. A headset icon appears. With INTERNAL AUDIO the hand mic stays silent.

    Notes

    • Your notes say "SCAN 3 times". SCAN is printed above GR (ALT + GR opens scan). The key that backs out is ESC, the one under LOCK.
    • ESC while a field is dark throws the change away. Always confirm with ENT first.
    • ALT + ESC locks the keypad (box with an X); the same again unlocks it. ALT + ENT turns the backlight on and off.
    • GR picks the channel group. Stay on G01, or the switch selects a different channel.
    • Knobs: drag them, tap their right half to turn clockwise and their left half counterclockwise, or tap a number. Z (panic zeroize) needs the side interlock and is never used here.
    • On a computer: arrow keys = ▲/▼, Enter = ENT, Esc = ESC, M = MODE, G = GR. Hold A (or Alt/Option) for ALT, for example A + ↑ = ALT + ▲.
